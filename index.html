Welcome to the Hollow

A gentle world, made for slowing down

Tucked between rolling clover fields and a babbling brook lies Clover Hollow, a sleepy valley waiting for someone to call it home. That someone is you.

Create your very own bunny — pick the ears, the fur, the freckles — and spend your days however you like. Plant a garden, bake a pie for the neighbors, or simply sit by the pond and watch the fireflies come out.

  • No timers, no pressure
  • A world that grows with you
  • Endlessly customizable bunny
